## A place to show the process

I want this website to be more than a collection of polished project screenshots. I want to document my real learning journey: what I'm trying to build, which concepts confuse me, what I figure out, and how my thinking changes.

## What I plan to write about

- Cybersecurity labs and detection ideas.
- Software projects, design decisions, and debugging.
- Skills I am studying and how I practice them.
- Milestones, mistakes, and lessons from the journey.

## What comes next

My goal is to write regularly, even when the update is small. **Consistency matters more than pretending every day is a breakthrough.**
